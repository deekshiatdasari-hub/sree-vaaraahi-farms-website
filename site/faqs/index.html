Frequently asked questions

Can't find what you're looking for? WhatsApp us or get in touch. We're happy to help.

Ordering and delivery

When do you deliver?

Every Wednesday and Saturday, across Hyderabad. You can order any time; an order placed on a delivery day goes to the next delivery day.

Is there a minimum order?

No. You can order any amount.

How much does delivery cost?

Delivery is free on orders of ₹1,000 or more. For smaller orders, you pay the Rapido delivery fare directly to the rider when your order arrives. The fare depends on the distance to your address.

Which areas do you deliver to?

Hyderabad, Secunderabad and Cyberabad (pincodes starting with 500), plus nearby areas including Patancheru. If you're not sure, WhatsApp us before ordering.

Can I order on both fruits and vegetables together?

Yes. Fruits, vegetables, greens, dairy, oils and groceries all go in the same cart and arrive together.

Payment

How do I pay?

All orders are paid in advance, by UPI, debit or credit card, net banking or wallet. We don't offer cash on delivery.

Why are your prices higher than the market?

Our produce is grown with care for the soil rather than with chemical fertilisers and pesticides. These methods take more time, effort and resources, and smaller, sustainable yields mean less produce per acre.

We believe the taste, nutrition and benefit to your health are worth it, and we invite you to experience the difference for yourself.

Changes, cancellations and refunds

Can I cancel my order?

Yes, until 8 PM the day before your delivery day, for a full refund. For a Wednesday delivery, cancel by 8 PM on Tuesday. Message or call us on WhatsApp at +91 76718 25610 with your order number.

What if something arrives damaged or missing?

Within 4 hours of delivery, send us your order number and a photo on WhatsApp. We'll refund that item or replace it in your next delivery, whichever you prefer.

When will I get my refund?

Approved refunds go back to your original payment method within 7 working days. Your bank may take 2–3 more days to show it. See the full Cancellation & Refund Policy.

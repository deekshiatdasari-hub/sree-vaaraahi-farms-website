Shipping & Delivery Policy

Effective from 18 November 2026

We deliver across Hyderabad every Wednesday and Saturday. Delivery is free on orders of ₹1,000 or more.

Where we deliver

We deliver to Hyderabad, Secunderabad and Cyberabad (pincodes starting with 500), plus nearby areas including Patancheru. The app checks your pincode before you pay, so you're never charged for an address we can't reach. On the website, please check with us first if you're unsure.

Delivery days

There's no cutoff time. Orders placed on a delivery day go to the next delivery day.

Delivery charges

Order valueDelivery charge
₹1,000 or moreFree
Under ₹1,000The Rapido delivery fare, paid by you directly to the rider in cash or UPI when the order arrives

The Rapido fare depends on the distance to your address. It is not included in the amount you pay online.

Receiving your order

  • The rider will call you before arriving. Please keep your phone reachable and make sure someone is available at the address.
  • Check your items when they arrive. Report any damaged, spoiled, missing or wrong items within 4 hours of delivery (see our Cancellation & Refund Policy).
  • Fresh produce can't be held or sent again. If the rider can't reach you, or the address is wrong, the order can't be refunded.

Tracking and delays

  • Tracking: the app shows each order's status (Confirmed, then Delivered). Our team can update you on WhatsApp at +91 76718 25610.
  • Delays: if heavy rain, strikes or other events outside our control delay a delivery, we'll tell you as early as we can and deliver on the next possible day, or refund you if you prefer.

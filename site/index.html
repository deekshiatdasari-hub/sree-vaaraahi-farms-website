All FAQs →
When do you deliver?

Every Wednesday and Saturday, across Hyderabad. You can order any time; an order placed on a delivery day goes to the next one.

Is there a minimum order or a delivery charge?

There's no minimum order. Delivery is free on orders of ₹1,000 or more. For smaller orders, you pay the Rapido delivery fare directly to the rider.

Which areas do you deliver to?

Hyderabad, Secunderabad and Cyberabad (pincodes starting with 500), plus nearby areas including Patancheru. Not sure? WhatsApp us.

What if something arrives damaged?

Send us a photo on WhatsApp within 4 hours of delivery. We'll refund that item or replace it in your next delivery. See our Cancellation & Refund Policy.

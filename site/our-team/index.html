The core team

Meet the heartbeat of Sree Vaaraahi Farms

Our core team is passionate about bringing you the purest, most nutritious produce. Meet the people behind the farm who are committed to nourishing your life and the planet.

Our mission

Wholesome food for 5,000 Hyderabad families

Sree Vaaraahi Farms brings naturally grown produce from the forest regions of Araku, Chintapalli and Paderu to homes across Hyderabad. Our goal is to make pure, wholesome food available to 5,000 families, especially people recovering from illness who need it most.

Every order is hand-picked, packed fresh and delivered every Wednesday and Saturday.

Anitha Yerra

Founder

Smt. Anitha Yerra, the visionary founder of Sree Vaaraahi Farms, is dedicated to cultivating nutritious food for society while driving positive change. She encourages eco-friendly farming practices that ensure chemical-free produce, promoting community health and environmental sustainability.

Beyond agriculture, she feeds and supports the poor in forest areas. Through her real estate company, Sree Shubhada Ventures, she empowers rural women with employment and financial independence, and she sponsors training programmes for young athletes.

Prakash Sunkara

Co-Founder

With two decades in digital marketing, Prakash has trained over 10,000 people through AP skill development programmes and private institutions. He left the digital and IT field to focus on farming after seeing the harm chemical farming does to health and the environment.

His vision is to provide wholesome vegetables to at least 5,000 people in Hyderabad, particularly those recovering from illness, and he works tirelessly to make sure quality food reaches them.

Santosh CH

Co-Founder

Santosh volunteered to join the mission of making food chemical-free for at least 5,000 people in Hyderabad. Alongside his job at a bank, he oversees administration and logistics, keeping operations smooth and deliveries on time.

In the first six weeks after the company began, his efforts brought on 464 customers.

Krishnam Raju

Director

Krishnam Raju runs administration and procurement. Drawing on his wide network, he sources the finest vegetables across Andhra Pradesh and Telangana so customers receive top-quality produce.

He shares the vision of chemical-free food for society, and his dedication has earned many testimonials and blessings from customers.

Chaitanya CH

Associate Director

Chaitanya ensures the highest standards of quality and good farming practice. She oversees yield grading and makes sure the farm follows sustainable, eco-friendly methods.

She also manages people and procurement, working with other small farmers to make sure they follow the same careful practices.

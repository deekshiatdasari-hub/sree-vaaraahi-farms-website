How it works

Fresh produce in three easy steps

Get naturally grown fruits, vegetables and pantry staples delivered to your door, every Wednesday and Saturday.

  1. Fill your basket

    Browse fruits, vegetables, leafy greens, dairy, wood-pressed oils and groceries, and add what you like. There's no minimum order.

  2. Pick a delivery day

    Choose Wednesday or Saturday. You can order any time; an order placed on a delivery day goes to the next one.

  3. We harvest and deliver

    We hand-pick and pack your order fresh, and the rider calls you before arriving at your door.

Where do you deliver?

Across Hyderabad, Secunderabad and Cyberabad (pincodes starting with 500), plus nearby areas including Patancheru. Not sure about your area? WhatsApp us and we'll tell you.

What if something isn't right?

Check your items when they arrive. If anything is damaged, spoiled, missing or wrong, send us a photo on WhatsApp within 4 hours of delivery and we'll refund it or replace it in your next delivery. Read the full Cancellation & Refund Policy.

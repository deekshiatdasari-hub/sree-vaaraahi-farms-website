Sree Vaaraahi Farms brings naturally grown produce from the forest regions of Andhra Pradesh to homes across Hyderabad.

### Where our produce comes from

Our fruits, vegetables, leafy greens and forest specials come from farms in **Araku**, **Chintapalli** and **Paderu**, in the hills of the Eastern Ghats, and from partner farms. They're grown without chemical fertilisers and pesticides, hand-picked, and brought straight to your door.

We also offer cold-pressed oils, dairy products and everyday groceries.

### Our mission

We started with a simple goal: to make pure, chemical-free food available to 5,000 families in Hyderabad, especially people recovering from illness who need it most.

### Who we are

Sree Vaaraahi Farms was founded by Prakash Sunkara. Read his story on our [About us](https://www.sreevaaraahifarms.in/our-team/) page.

### How it works

- Order on our website or the Sree Vaaraahi Farms app, and pay online.
- We deliver every **Wednesday and Saturday** across Hyderabad.
- Delivery is free on orders of ₹1,000 or more.

*Effective from 18 November 2026*

Our produce is harvested and packed for each delivery day, so we can't take back fresh items once they're delivered. If something arrives damaged, spoiled, missing or wrong, we will refund or replace it.

### At a glance

| Situation | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| You cancel before 8 PM the day before your delivery day | Full refund |
| You cancel after 8 PM the day before your delivery day | Not possible: the order is already being packed |
| We cancel your order, or an item turns out to be unavailable | Full refund for the cancelled items |
| An item is damaged, spoiled, missing or wrong, and you tell us within 4 hours of delivery | Refund for that item, or a replacement in your next delivery |
| Payment was deducted but the order was not confirmed | Automatic full refund |
| You changed your mind after delivery | No refund (perishable goods) |
| Delivery failed because nobody was reachable or the address was wrong | No refund (perishable goods) |

### How to cancel an order

Message or call us on WhatsApp at +91 76718 25610 with your order number before 8 PM the day before your delivery day. Example: for a Wednesday delivery, cancel by 8 PM on Tuesday.

### How to report a problem with your order

1. Within **4 hours of delivery**, send us on WhatsApp at +91 76718 25610: your order number, a photo of the item, and a short description.
2. We reply the same day and offer a refund for that item or a replacement in your next delivery, whichever you prefer.

Small variations in size, colour, shape or weight (up to 10%) are natural for farm produce and are not counted as damage.

### When you get your money back

- **Approved refunds** go back to your original payment method (UPI, card, net banking or wallet) within **7 working days**. Your bank may take 2–3 more days to show it.
- **Automatic refunds** for failed or unconfirmed orders usually arrive within 5–7 working days.
- **Rapido delivery charge:** for orders under ₹1,000 you pay this directly to the rider, so we can't refund it. If a delivery fails because of a mistake on our side, we cover the cost of delivering again.

*Effective from 18 November 2026*

Our produce is harvested and packed for each delivery day, so we can't take back fresh items once they're delivered. If something arrives damaged, spoiled, missing or wrong, we will refund or replace it.

### At a glance

| Situation | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| You cancel before 8 PM the day before your delivery day | Full refund |
| You cancel after 8 PM the day before your delivery day | Not possible: the order is already being packed |
| We cancel your order, or an item turns out to be unavailable | Full refund for the cancelled items |
| An item is damaged, spoiled, missing or wrong, and you tell us within 4 hours of delivery | Refund for that item, or a replacement in your next delivery |
| Payment was deducted but the order was not confirmed | Automatic full refund |
| You changed your mind after delivery | No refund (perishable goods) |
| Delivery failed because nobody was reachable or the address was wrong | No refund (perishable goods) |

### How to cancel an order

Message or call us on WhatsApp at +91 76718 25610 with your order number before 8 PM the day before your delivery day. Example: for a Wednesday delivery, cancel by 8 PM on Tuesday.

### How to report a problem with your order

1. Within **4 hours of delivery**, send us on WhatsApp at +91 76718 25610: your order number, a photo of the item, and a short description.
2. We reply the same day and offer a refund for that item or a replacement in your next delivery, whichever you prefer.

Small variations in size, colour, shape or weight (up to 10%) are natural for farm produce and are not counted as damage.

### When you get your money back

- **Approved refunds** go back to your original payment method (UPI, card, net banking or wallet) within **7 working days**. Your bank may take 2–3 more days to show it.
- **Automatic refunds** for failed or unconfirmed orders usually arrive within 5–7 working days.
- **Rapido delivery charge:** for orders under ₹1,000 you pay this directly to the rider, so we can't refund it. If a delivery fails because of a mistake on our side, we cover the cost of delivering again.

### Contact

WhatsApp or call +91 76718 25610, or email hello@sreevaaraahifarms.in. Unresolved complaints can be taken to our Grievance Officer, listed in our [Terms & Conditions](https://www.sreevaaraahifarms.in/terms-and-conditions/).

*Effective from 18 November 2026*

We deliver across Hyderabad every **Wednesday and Saturday**. Delivery is free on orders of ₹1,000 or more.

### Where we deliver

We deliver to Hyderabad, Secunderabad and Cyberabad (pincodes starting with 500), plus nearby areas including Patancheru. The app checks your pincode before you pay, so you're never charged for an address we can't reach. On the website, please check with us first if you're unsure.

### Delivery days

| You order on | You can choose |
| --- | --- |
| Sunday, Monday or Tuesday | Wednesday or Saturday |
| Wednesday | Saturday |
| Thursday or Friday | Saturday or the following Wednesday |
| Saturday | The following Wednesday |

There's no cutoff time. Orders placed on a delivery day go to the next delivery day.

### Delivery charges

| Order value | Delivery charge |
| --- | --- |
| ₹1,000 or more | Free |
| Under ₹1,000 | The Rapido delivery fare, paid by you directly to the rider in cash or UPI when the order arrives |

The Rapido fare depends on the distance to your address. It is not included in the amount you pay online.

### Receiving your order

- The rider will call you before arriving. Please keep your phone reachable and make sure someone is available at the address.
- Check your items when they arrive. Report any damaged, spoiled, missing or wrong items within 4 hours of delivery (see our [Cancellation & Refund Policy](https://www.sreevaaraahifarms.in/refund-policy/)).
- Fresh produce can't be held or sent again. If the rider can't reach you, or the address is wrong, the order can't be refunded.

### Tracking and delays

- **Tracking:** the app shows each order's status (Confirmed, then Delivered). Our team can update you on WhatsApp at +91 76718 25610.
- **Delays:** if heavy rain, strikes or other events outside our control delay a delivery, we'll tell you as early as we can and deliver on the next possible day, or refund you if you prefer.

Sree Vaaraahi Farms brings naturally grown produce from the forest regions of Andhra Pradesh to homes across Hyderabad.

### Where our produce comes from

Our fruits, vegetables, leafy greens and forest specials come from farms in **Araku**, **Chintapalli** and **Paderu**, in the hills of the Eastern Ghats, and from partner farms. They're grown without chemical fertilisers and pesticides, hand-picked, and brought straight to your door.

We also offer cold-pressed oils, dairy products and everyday groceries.

### Our mission

We started with a simple goal: to make pure, chemical-free food available to 5,000 families in Hyderabad, especially people recovering from illness who need it most.

### Who we are

Sree Vaaraahi Farms was founded by Smt. Anitha Yerra, with co-founders Prakash Sunkara and Santosh CH, and Krishnam Raju overseeing administration and procurement. Meet the team on our [Our Team](https://www.sreevaaraahifarms.in/our-team/) page.
